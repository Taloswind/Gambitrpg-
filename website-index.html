Do you dare Gambit on your next adventure?

A tabletop RPG played with a deck of cards. Your cards are your very life, and each one is a piece of your soul. The Dealer tells the story and knows what's coming next. You decide when to stop pushing your luck.

The game

No dice. No character sheet. Just the deck.

GambitRPG was built so anyone can sit down and play, at any age, without a stack of reading first. The rules are light, so the table's choices and the Dealer's story do the heavy lifting. Adventures from other systems adapt easily, and you don't need much space, which leaves room for snacks.

The deck splits in two. Players build characters from one half. The Dealer draws fate from the other.

How to play

Every check is a gamble.

The Dealer calls a check whenever an action could fail. Each one has a color, physical or mental, and a difficulty: the number of successes you need.

Easy

Red: climb a rope
Black: hide from a patrol

Normal

Red: attack an enemy
Black: spot an ambush

Hard

Red: break down a solid door
Black: disarm a deadly trap

Very Hard

Red: carry a friend back to town
Black: turn an enemy to your side

Impossible

Red: topple a stone statue
Black: talk down the big boss

Step 1

The deal

The Dealer deals cards one at a time. Assign each one to a card of yours before the next comes.

Step 2

Match

A card that matches the check's color or one of your face values is a success. Doubles score twice.

Step 3

Blight or fold

A miss is a failure. Flip one of your cards to blight it and keep going, or accept the failure and stop.

Step 4

Push your luck

Passed? Take one more draw, all or nothing. Win and the extra successes boost your result. Lose and the whole check fails.

Joker

The Reaper

Ends the check in failure, whatever you've already drawn. Then the Dealer reshuffles.

Joker

The Jester

Ends the check as a pass, and your current successes count as extras. Then the Dealer reshuffles.

Blighted

Flipped over. Fatigue or stress locks the card until it's restored.

Injured

A blight turned sideways. Much harder to heal.

Down or dead

All five blighted and you're disabled. All five injured and your character dies.

The Dealer
The Dealer may know your upcoming fate, but is never allowed to change the order of the draw.GambitRPG Rule Book

The Dealer narrates, sets the stakes and taunts you about the next card. The Dealer's Handbook covers quick encounter building, legendary actions for bosses and item-card loot. There's no currency to track.
